# Underneath

A Looter/Metroidvania game where you play as a Mother Raccoon struggling to survive in the urban underworld. Navigate through the slums, dumpster dive for valuable items, and work to reunite with your family.

Created for the July 2024 Ignite Game Jam hosted by P1. You venture into various locations throughout the slums, scavenging for items to sell in order to survive and eventually meet your family again.

## Key Features
- Metroidvania exploration with interconnected areas
- Looter mechanics - scavenge and sell items for survival
- Emotional narrative about family and perseverance
- Environmental storytelling through detailed slum environments

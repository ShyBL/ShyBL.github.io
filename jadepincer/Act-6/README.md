# The Emerald Championship

Date: 1023

**The magisters face their final test at the Kakita Dueling Academy and participate in the Emerald Championship, the culmination of their journey.**

The act begins at the Kakita Dueling Academy, where Akahiro undergoes his rank test under the guidance of Kakita Toshimoko, the Kenshinzen and Grandmaster of the Duelist School. This test represents not just a personal milestone for Akahiro, but also a crucial step in the magisters' preparation for the challenges ahead.
<br><br>
Following the academy, the magisters travel to the Tortoise lands on an Emerald Mission, where they encounter unique challenges and learn more about the minor clans' role in the empire's political landscape. Their adventures in Otosan Uchi serve as a prelude to the main event - the Emerald Championship itself.
<br><br>
The Emerald Championship represents the ultimate test of the magisters' skills, diplomacy, and resolve. It is here that all their training, alliances, and discoveries come together as they face the final challenge of preventing the Clan War while proving their worth as Emerald Magisters. 
<br><br>
Throughout this journey, Shika Kazuki has ingrained herself in the workings of the imperial family, keeping herself useful to be called again to the aid of the emerald magistrate, acting as a go-between for Kuni Genji and Kakita Akahiro to the courts.

# Voyage with Shiba Sono

Date: 1023

**The magisters embark on a voyage with Shiba Sono, using scrying to track the Clan War while navigating imperial politics.**

The magisters set sail with Shiba Sono, the fire shugenja and courtier turned jade magister, who serves as their supernatural mentor. Their journey takes them across the empire as they use mystical scrying techniques to track the movements and plans of those seeking to ignite the Clan War.
<br><br>
On the first day, they perform scrying rituals in the water to locate the source of the conflict. The second day brings them to the Kyotai Castle's plains of battle, where they encounter Deathseekers and witness the brutal reality of clan warfare. The castle becomes the site of a crucial wedding between imperial families, attended by the magisters on behalf of the Otomo family.
<br><br>
During their seven-day stay at Kyotai Castle, the magisters witness the birth of a Child of the Fortune of Mountain Wind, an event of great spiritual significance. The act culminates in a dramatic battle at Kyotai Castle, where the magisters must balance their duty to maintain peace with the harsh realities of clan conflict.

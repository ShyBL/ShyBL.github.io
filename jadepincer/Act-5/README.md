# Emerald Champion Castle II

Date: 1023

**The magisters return to the Emerald Champion Castle to visit imperial families and prepare for the final phase of their mission.**

The magisters return to the Emerald Champion Castle for a fourteen-day stay, during which they visit various imperial families and strengthen their connections within the imperial court. This period serves as both a respite from their travels and an opportunity to gather intelligence and build alliances that will be crucial for preventing the Clan War.
<br><br>
During their stay, they interact with key imperial figures including Miya Sanjuro, the Imperial Bureaucrat and artisan, and Otomo Eisai, the Emerald Magister. They also meet Otomo Koryusai, Senior Event Organizer for the Imperial Herald, and Otomo Masashi, an avid card player and imperial attendant.
<br><br>
The magisters use this time to consolidate their findings from their travels and prepare for the final phase of their mission. They must balance their duty to the empire with the complex web of clan politics they have encountered, while also preparing for the upcoming Emerald Championship that will test their skills and resolve.

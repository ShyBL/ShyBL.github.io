# The Topaz Championship

Date: November 15, 2023

**The Topaz Championship is disrupted by mysterious sabotage, leading the new Topaz Champion and his allies to the Emerald Champion Castle.**

The 1023 Topaz Championship begins with the usual pageantry and ceremony, but is soon disrupted by mysterious sabotage during the Grand Melee. The young Togashi Akai, with the assistance of several other competitors, manages to capture the saboteur and is declared Topaz Champion.
<br><br>
Following the championship, Akai and those who assisted him are summoned to the Emerald Champion Castle. Meanwhile, Akahiro plans to visit his family but is also summoned to the castle, while Kazuki decides to embark on a journey of his own.
<br><br>
At a waystation along the road, Akahiro meets Akai and they face a dangerous Ningenyo together. They also encounter a mysterious emerald magister woman who challenges them to a duel, revealing the complex web of politics and supernatural threats that await them.

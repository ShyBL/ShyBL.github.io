**Story Breakdown**

Act I \- In the Palace of the Emerald Champion

Big Players \- Doji Satsume, Agasha Sumiko, Bayushi Yojiro, Kakita Yoshi.  
Small Players \- Crane Clan, Imperial Families, Kakita Toshimoko.

Themes \- Castle Court Intrigue, Learning Curve, Personal Growth, Bonds.  
Big Picture Goals \- Jade Magistrate Allies.

Big Locations \- Emerald Champion Castle, Capital CIty.   
Small Locations \- Kyotai Castle, Ninkentoshi, Kakita Dueling Academy, Tortoise clan lands.

Big Events \- Topaz Championship, Emerald Championship, Winter Court.

Small events \-

* Doji Satsume dies.  
* The Scorpion Clan disrupts the Topaz Championship.  
* The Lion Clan wage war against the Crane, in a campaign across the Fields of Battle.  
* Unicron Clan break a treaty with the Lion, over land and marriage.  
* Mirumoto Hitomi kills the Crab Champion’s youngest son in a duel.  
* The Prefect Land shinseist sect is banned in Phoenix Clan lands.

* The imperial Otomo family asks the magisters to attend a wedding on their behalf, and maintain balance between the clans.  
  * The Lion Clan attack Kyotai Castle and Toshi Ranbo. Lion Champion dies at the hand of the Crane Champion.  
  * The Itagawa family are forced to choose between two candidates for the clan’s leadership.  
* The “Miya Blessing” parade across the empire before the winter.

**Agasha Sumiko, Ruby Champion**. Her duty is to train new magisters and to act as second in command to the Emerald Champion. She is Acting Emerald Champion after the death of Doji Satsume.

**Bayushi Yojiro, Honest Scorpion.** Senior Emerald Magister with high ambitions. He aims to navigate between the underlying clan agenda and the demanding matters of the imperial court.

**Kakita Yoshi, Voice of Honor.** Esteemed Imperial Chancellor, and Daimyō. Aims to use cultural advancement in city centers across the empire, to gather momentum for a more centralized government, by strengthening imperial presence in the up-and-coming cities.

**Tsume no Doji Takashi,** Young Daimyo of Kyotei Castle.   
**Nasu no Shiba Shizuma**, Former General of Daimyo’s father, Tsume Retsu.  
**Tsume Kotonoha,** Young captain of the guard. **Tsume Yemon**, head ley-priest.

**Shiba Itsuyo**, warrior turned princess.   
**Shiba Katsuda** and Lady Chisei, the Lord and Lady of Nikesake, City of Treaties.

**Asako Maezawa,** Phoenix Clan Champion Shiba Tsukune’s newest personal advisor  
**Doji Kuzunobu,** Fox Minor Clan member and husband of Doji Hotaru, Crane Clan Champion.

**Damasu no Akodo Maeda**, lover of Takashi and murderer of Retsu. Former daughter of the Damasu Daimyo at Kyotei Castle. Former Geisha.   
**Itto, Deathseeker** & former ronin.

**Itagawa Kenpu, veteran craftsman**. Served in the capital city’s guard on the Miwaku Kabe, The Enchanted Wall, with his retinue.  
**Akodo Ryojin, Estate Commander (Chui)**. Served on the Miwaku Kabe as retinue.  
**Itagawa Kintaro,** young Chui in the Akodo legions, Kenpu’s nephew.   
**Itagawa Yugozohime,** Young Governess to be (of Hirosaka, Lion Champion personal holding).

***Juan Sánchez-Villalobos Ramírez,*** Chief Metallurgist to the King of Mewenae. As a boy genius, he worked as a surgeon on a gaijin ship during the Battle of White Stag, now he's a trader of goods, trying to get by.

**Miya Sanjuro, Imperial Bureaucrat** and artisan.  
**Otomo Eisai, Emerald Magister.**  
**Otomo Koryusai,** Senior Event Organizer for the Imperial Herald.  
**Otomo Masashi,** avid card player and imperial attendant.

**Seppun Hokuto**, Instructor, famous artist, and investigator.  
**Hiruma Shigeki, Shadowlands Advisor** in the Palace of the Emerald Champion.  
**Hida Osa-no**, His nephew, Emerald Legioneer.  
**Shosuro Koharu**, Emerald Magister in City of Lies \- Ryokō Owari Toshi  
His family is moving to Otosan Uchi.

**Shinjo Akib, Castellan of the Palace of the Emerald Champion.**

**Kamikaze no Seppun no Arashi**, of the Imperial navy, married to a seppun healer-woman. Born of the Mantis Minor Clan.

**Otomo Sutō, of the Dragon Storyteller School.** His grandmother is a Shika Matchmaker of the Deer Minor Clan.

**Otomo Rintarô**, Shika Kazuki’s bithroed, who works for the Imperial Herald.

**Shika Ogasha**, grand master of the matchmaking school. 

**Kuni Yori,** Daimyō.   
**Kuni Shibokuna,** Grandmaster of the Purifiers witch-hunter order. Yori's father and Kuni (Shibamaru) Genji’s mentor.

**Kakita Toshimoko, Kenshinzen.** Brother-in-law to Doji Satsume. Grandmaster of the Duelist School, and Utaku no Kakita Akahiro’s mentor.

**Shiba Sono**, fire shugenja and courtier, turned jade magister. Supernatural mentor to the up and coming Emerald Magisters.

**Kitsu Sokori,** mysterious slomen courtier who’s suspected to be a Mahō-tsukai.

# Lion Lands

Date: 1023

**The magisters travel to the Lion Lands to maintain balance between the clans and investigate the growing tensions.**

The magisters arrive in the Lion Lands, staying at the Emerald Embassy in Ninkentoshi for three to five days. Their mission is to maintain balance between the clans and investigate the growing tensions that threaten to erupt into full-scale war. The Lion Clan's aggressive stance against the Crane has created a volatile situation that requires delicate diplomatic handling.
<br><br>
During their stay, they encounter key Lion figures including Itagawa Kenpu, the veteran craftsman who served on the Miwaku Kabe (The Enchanted Wall), and his retinue. They also meet Akodo Ryojin, Estate Commander (Chui), and Itagawa Kintaro, a young Chui in the Akodo legions who is Kenpu's nephew.
<br><br>
The magisters must navigate the complex politics of the Lion Clan, particularly the Itagawa family's internal struggle to choose between two candidates for clan leadership. The situation is further complicated by the Lion Clan's recent attacks on Kyotai Castle and Toshi Ranbo, which resulted in the death of the Lion Champion at the hands of the Crane Champion.

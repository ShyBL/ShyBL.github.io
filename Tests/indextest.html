A mysterious robot has landed on Earth on a quest to find monuments and other landmarks. But what is its purpose? Only time will tell...
This beat 'em up style game features punching action, breakable containers, loot, and four levels (France, Italy, Egypt, USA) to discover cultural landmarks. The game is styled in low-poly 3D with a full-screen pixelation effect, giving a modern take to a classic style and genre.
Created for Summer Game Jam 2026. Future updates may include additional move sets, weapons, particle effects, and a landmark scanning feature.
  • Beat 'em up combat with punching action and breakable containers
  • Four diverse levels: France, Italy, Egypt, and USA
  • Cultural landmark discovery and exploration
  • Low-poly 3D art style with pixelation effects
  • Loot collection system

A Looter/Metroidvania game where you play as a Mother Raccoon struggling to survive in the urban underworld. Navigate through the slums, dumpster dive for valuable items, and work to reunite with your family.
Created for the July 2024 Ignite Game Jam hosted by P1. You venture into various locations throughout the slums, scavenging for items to sell in order to survive and eventually meet your family again.
  • Metroidvania exploration with interconnected areas
  • Looter mechanics - scavenge and sell items for survival
  • Emotional narrative about family and perseverance
  • Environmental storytelling through detailed slum environments

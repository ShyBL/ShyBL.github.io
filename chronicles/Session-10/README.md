# The Emperor's Judgment

Date: Winter Court, 1158

The characters are summoned to the Imperial Court to face the Emperor's judgment for their actions throughout the year. But this is no simple trial - the fate of the entire Empire hangs in the balance as ancient prophecies come to fruition.

## Characters
- Kakita Haruto - Crane Duelist
- Hida Masa - Crab Berserker
- Isawa Yuki - Phoenix Shugenja
- Bayushi Kage - Scorpion Courtier
- Akodo Shin - Lion Tactician
- Mirumoto Ryu - Dragon Swordsman
- Yoritomo Kenji - Mantis Merchant
- Togashi Sora - Dragon Monk
- Hida Taro - Crab Commander
- Isawa Michiko - Phoenix Elemental Master
- Akodo Takeshi - Lion General
- Doji Hotaru - Crane Champion
- The Emperor Hantei

## Locations
- Imperial Palace
- Throne Room
- Secret Council Chambers
- Sacred Imperial Gardens

## Key Events
- Arrival at the Imperial Court for final judgment
- Confrontation with all the major clan leaders
- The revelation of the true purpose of their journey
- The Emperor's final decision that will change the Empire forever

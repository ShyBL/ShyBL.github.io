# The Phoenix's Fire

Date: Mid Autumn, 1158

The characters travel to the Phoenix lands to investigate reports of strange magical phenomena. What they discover there could either save the Empire or destroy it, as ancient elemental forces are awakening.

## Characters
- Kakita Haruto - Crane Duelist
- Hida Masa - Crab Berserker
- Isawa Yuki - Phoenix Shugenja
- Bayushi Kage - Scorpion Courtier
- Akodo Shin - Lion Tactician
- Mirumoto Ryu - Dragon Swordsman
- Yoritomo Kenji - Mantis Merchant
- Togashi Sora - Dragon Monk
- Hida Taro - Crab Commander
- Isawa Michiko - Phoenix Elemental Master

## Locations
- Phoenix Clan Library
- Elemental Temples
- Sacred Grove of the Elements
- Ancient Shrine of the Five Rings

## Key Events
- Arrival during a magical storm of unprecedented power
- Discovery of ancient elemental spirits awakening
- Ritual to commune with the elemental forces
- The choice between power and balance that will affect the entire Empire

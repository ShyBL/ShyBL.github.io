# In the Emerald Champion Castle

Date: November 29, 2023

**Following the championnship, the characters are instructed to go to Shiro Yogasha, The Castle of the Emerald Champion, to begin their newly appointed roles.**

*On their journey, they encounter a caravan of foreigners, supposedly from the Unicron Clan, led by an enigmatic trader from Merenae. The caravan's "Baba" uses forbidden magic involving a dog's skull to warn them of terrible danger ahead. Leading a scouting party, the characters then encounter angry mermen named Ningyo.*

*At the crossroads, they meet a strange old woman, an Emerald Magister incharge of the way-station, who challenges them to a duel and kills Asahina Daijin. The magister is revealed to be an animal-spirit in disguise, a Tengu, who subsequently disappears. The real, bewildered, and exhausted magister then appears in its place.*


*At the Castle of the Emerald Champion, Isawa Mihagwa receives lessons alongside other fledgling emerald magisters. He encounters various strange occurrences that lead him to explore the unnatural happenings in the castle. He discovers that some are truely, evil or corrupt spirits and banishes them. Other occurrences are due to the unruly Scorpion Shugenja, an emerald magister fledgling, who is under curfew not to use her strange powers.*

# The Crab's Duty

Date: Early Autumn, 1158

The characters are called to the Wall to assist the Crab Clan in defending against a massive Shadowlands incursion. But the real threat may not be from the creatures beyond the Wall, but from within the ranks of the defenders themselves.

## Characters
- Kakita Haruto - Crane Duelist
- Hida Masa - Crab Berserker
- Isawa Yuki - Phoenix Shugenja
- Bayushi Kage - Scorpion Courtier
- Akodo Shin - Lion Tactician
- Mirumoto Ryu - Dragon Swordsman
- Yoritomo Kenji - Mantis Merchant
- Togashi Sora - Dragon Monk
- Hida Taro - Crab Commander

## Locations
- The Great Wall of the North
- Crab Clan Fortress
- Shadowlands Border
- Underground Command Center

## Key Events
- Arrival at the Wall during a massive attack
- Discovery of corruption among the Crab leadership
- Desperate defense against Shadowlands creatures
- The revelation of a traitor within the Crab ranks

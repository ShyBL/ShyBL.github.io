# The Emperor's Tournament

Date: Late Spring, 1158

**The characters are invited to participate in a grand tournament held in honor of the Emperor's birthday.** *But beneath the pageantry and competition lies a web of clan politics and hidden agendas. As the tournament progresses, it becomes clear that more than just honor is at stake.*

## Characters
- Kakita Haruto - Crane Duelist
- Hida Masa - Crab Berserker
- Isawa Yuki - Phoenix Shugenja
- Bayushi Kage - Scorpion Courtier
- Akodo Shin - Lion Tactician
- Mirumoto Ryu - Dragon Swordsman

## Locations
- Imperial Tournament Grounds
- Pavilion of the Clans
- Gardens of Contemplation
- The Emperor's Viewing Platform

## Key Events
- Opening ceremony blessing by the Emperor himself
- Haruto's victory in the dueling competition against a Dragon champion
- Midnight conspiracy meeting in the gardens
- The Emperor's final judgment reveals the true purpose of the tournament

# The Crane's Honor

Date: Early Winter, 1158

The characters return to Crane lands to investigate a scandal that threatens to destroy the Crane Clan's reputation. As they navigate the complex world of court politics, they must decide what honor truly means.

## Characters
- Kakita Haruto - Crane Duelist
- Hida Masa - Crab Berserker
- Isawa Yuki - Phoenix Shugenja
- Bayushi Kage - Scorpion Courtier
- Akodo Shin - Lion Tactician
- Mirumoto Ryu - Dragon Swordsman
- Yoritomo Kenji - Mantis Merchant
- Togashi Sora - Dragon Monk
- Hida Taro - Crab Commander
- Isawa Michiko - Phoenix Elemental Master
- Akodo Takeshi - Lion General
- Doji Hotaru - Crane Champion

## Locations
- Crane Clan Court
- Dueling Grounds
- Secret Tea Houses
- Ancestral Gardens

## Key Events
- Arrival at court during a major scandal
- Investigation of the dishonorable accusations
- Duel to defend the Crane Clan's honor
- The revelation of the true nature of honor and duty

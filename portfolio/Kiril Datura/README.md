# Kiril Datura
A 2D side-scrolling Metroidvania action platformer set in Latifundia, a world inspired by 16th-century Romania.
<br>
Players explore a non-linear open map as Kiril — son of a shaman and brewer of Da'j, a potion of legendary power. When General Dărvan Ardelean leads the Latifundian army to steal the formula, kidnaps Kiril's father Bakro, and burns their village, Kiril sets out for revenge. New areas, abilities, and secrets are unlocked through boss defeats and hidden item discovery across melee, ranged, and movement skill trees.

## Key Features
- Non-linear open map with interconnected zones unlocked through progression
- Melee, ranged, and movement abilities acquired through items and boss battles
- Rich lore rooted in 16th-century Romanian mythology and culture
- Original antagonist and narrative — from village origins to a full revenge arc
- Two distinct audio environments powered by FMOD adaptive audio
